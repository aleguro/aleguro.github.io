odsl
A fluent DSL for Ruby service objects

Built to solve the "giant call method" problem in Rails. Odsl turns complex business workflows into a sequence of named, audited, resumable steps — with built-in immutability, background jobs, distributed locking, and a step-level audit log that lets you resume a failed run exactly where it left off.
